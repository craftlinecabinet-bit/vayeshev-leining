# Vayeshev Leining Trainer

A phone app for learning to lein Parshas Vayeshev (Bereishis 37:1–40:23) and the Shabbos Chanukah maftir (Bamidbar 7:1–17), Ashkenazi.

- **Practice**: spaced-repetition quizzes graded right or wrong — reading from the bare scroll letters, trop, vowels, stress (mil'eil / milra), sheva na / nach, look-alike words, what comes next, and aliyah breaks.
- **Car**: hands-free. You hear the cue, lein the pasuk, and the app scores your tune against Rabbi Chaim Fessel's recording, then listens for "good" or "again".
- **Tikkun**: the full text with all marks, no trop, no vowels, or scroll view; tap any word for its trop, stress and sheva.
- **Audio**: load Rabbi Fessel's recordings (from TorahDownloads) once per aliyah; the app finds each pasuk on its own.

Open it in Chrome on Android and use "Add to Home screen" to install it.
